Shipping & Delivery Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Last Updated: November 2026

1. Transparent Shipping Rates & Thresholds

Every couture piece dispatched from the Artpoure atelier travels fully insured against transit loss or damage. Our domestic shipping fee structure is unified across both our mobile app and website:

Orders of ₹2,999 and above: FREE INSURED EXPRESS SHIPPING
Orders below ₹2,999: ₹149 Flat Insured Shipping

The calculated shipping charge is transparently reflected on your Cart and Checkout order breakdown before payment authorization.

2. Atelier Dispatch Timelines

Because our collections feature both ready masterweaves and bespoke commissions, dispatch timeframes vary as follows:

  • Ready-to-Ship Heirlooms (Standard Catalogue): Dispatched within 2 to 4 business days following payment verification or COD confirmation.
  • Hand-Painted & ArtClusive Commissions (Made-to-Order): Hand-painted sarees and custom loom pieces require meticulous artisan curation and are dispatched within 7 to 14 business days.

3. Transit & Delivery Estimates

We partner exclusively with premium express courier networks—including Blue Dart, Delhivery Express, and DTDC Priority—to guarantee pan-India door-to-door delivery:

  • Metro Cities (Delhi-NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Kolkata, Chennai, Hyderabad): Delivery within 2 to 4 business days post-dispatch.
  • Tier-2 & Tier-3 Cities: Delivery within 4 to 7 business days post-dispatch.
  • Remote or Northeastern Regions: Delivery within 6 to 9 business days post-dispatch.

4. Live Tracking & Notifications

As soon as your shipment is handed over to our logistics partner, an automated dispatch notification with an Air Waybill (AWB) tracking link is transmitted to your registered WhatsApp number, SMS, and email. You can also monitor fulfillment status in real time inside the Order History section of the app and website.

5. Tamper-Evident Packaging & Silk Mark Certification

Every heirloom is enclosed within archival moisture-resistant wrapping, sealed inside a signature Artpoure rigid luxury box with a tamper-evident holographic seal, and accompanied by an authentic Silk Mark certificate. If the external courier pouch appears visibly tampered with or opened upon arrival, please do not accept the package and notify our concierge immediately at artpoure.official@gmail.com.

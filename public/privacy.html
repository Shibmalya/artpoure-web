Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Last Updated: November 2026

1. Commitment to Discretion & Privacy

At ARTPOURE ("Artpoure", "we", "our", or "us"), we craft bespoke, certified silk sarees, hand-painted heirlooms, and haute ethnic couture. Discretion and the safeguarding of our clients' personal privacy are foundational tenets of our atelier. This Privacy Policy discloses our practices concerning the collection, storage, processing, and protection of information gathered through the Artpoure Android mobile application and the web storefront at artpoure.in.

2. Personal Data We Collect

When you browse our collections, book bespoke consultations, or purchase handcrafted heirlooms, we may collect the following personal information:

  • Identity & Contact Details: Full name, telephone number (for order verification and dispatch updates), and email address.
  • Delivery & Logistics Information: House/flat number, building name, street, area, city, state, postal pincode, and optional GPS coordinates (utilized exclusively upon explicit user confirmation for doorstep courier address accuracy).
  • Bespoke Consultations & Order History: Records of couture heirlooms acquired, chosen color variants, custom handloom weave requests, and scheduled virtual loom consultation dates.
  • Style & Catalog Preferences: Optional shopping preference indicators (e.g., Women's or Men's collections) and wishlisted heirlooms to tailor recommendations in your "For You" feed.

3. Payment Security & Third-Party Processing

To provide seamless, industry-standard transactions, Artpoure integrates with certified, top-tier cloud and financial technology infrastructure:

  • Razorpay Software Private Limited: Online payment processing for UPI, Credit/Debit cards, NetBanking, and Partial-COD booking tokens.
  • Firebase & Google Cloud Platform (Google LLC): Secure client authentication (Firebase Auth), database persistence (Cloud Firestore), and encrypted media hosting (Firebase Storage).
STRICT PAYMENT CARD INDUSTRY (PCI-DSS) COMPLIANCE: Artpoure never collects, processes, or stores your raw credit card numbers, debit card numbers, CVVs, or UPI PINs on any of our servers or databases. All payment transactions occur directly within Razorpay's PCI-DSS Level 1 certified 256-bit encrypted gateway.

4. Purpose of Data Processing

We process your data exclusively for legitimate retail, craftsmanship, and compliance purposes:

  • Fulfilling, packing, and dispatching handcrafted couture orders with tamper-evident packaging and Silk Mark verification.
  • Communicating critical order status notifications, courier tracking numbers, and consultation confirmations via SMS, WhatsApp, and email.
  • Preventing fraudulent transactions and mitigating doorstep refusal risks (RTO) on Cash on Delivery orders.
  • Providing customer concierge support and managing warranty or handcrafted replacement inquiries.

5. Account & Personal Data Deletion

In compliance with Google Play Store User Data policies and applicable data protection legislation, you retain the absolute right to inspect, update, or completely delete your personal profile, delivery addresses, and account history.

HOW TO REQUEST DATA DELETION: To permanently delete your Artpoure client account and erase all associated personal data from our active databases, send an email from your registered email address to artpoure.official@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request". Please include your registered mobile number. Our privacy team will authenticate and permanently purge your data within thirty (30) business days, subject only to records required for statutory tax and accounting audits.

6. Data Retention & Security Standards

We implement robust technical and operational measures, including TLS 1.3 encryption in transit, strict Firestore access control rules limiting document access solely to verified account owners, and periodic security reviews to protect client confidentiality.

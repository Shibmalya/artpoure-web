LEGAL & COMPLIANCE

Returns, Refunds & Cancellations

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Last Updated: November 2026

1. Standard Collection: 7-Day Returns & Exchanges

We want you to be enchanted by your Artpoure purchase. For standard catalog pieces (non-bespoke catalog sarees, dupattas, and ready couture), we offer a hassle-free 7-day return and exchange window from the verified date of delivery.

  • Eligibility Criteria: The garment must be completely unused, unworn, unwashed, and unfragranced, with all original atelier tags, brand labels, and Silk Mark seals firmly intact.
  • Transit Damage Claims & Unboxing Video: In the rare event that an item arrives damaged or with a defect, customers must provide a clear, continuous unboxing video recorded from opening the sealed courier outer pouch. This ensures rapid dispatch of an identical replacement.

2. ArtClusive & Hand-Painted Commissions

MADE-TO-ORDER & 1-OF-1 BESPOKE PIECES: Hand-painted sarees, customized masterweaves, monogrammed pallus, and 1-of-1 ArtClusive commissions are created specifically to your personal specifications. Consequently, these bespoke creations are strictly non-returnable and non-refundable once crafting has commenced, unless the piece arrives damaged in transit or a completely incorrect item is delivered.

3. Order Cancellation Policy

Standard orders may be cancelled within 24 hours of placement without penalty, provided fulfillment or courier dispatch has not yet initiated. To cancel an order, navigate to your Order History in the Artpoure app or website, or email artpoure.official@gmail.com with your Order ID.

4. Partial-COD Booking Token Policy

To provide accessible Cash on Delivery while discouraging courier fraud and frivolous doorstep rejections, Artpoure utilizes a Partial-COD advance booking model:

  • When selecting Insured Cash on Delivery, a nominal advance booking token of ₹299 is collected upfront via Razorpay.
  • The advance booking token of ₹299 is deducted from your order grand total, leaving the remaining balance due upon delivery via cash or UPI.
  • Doorstep Rejection (RTO) Clause: If an order has been dispatched by our logistics team and the client subsequently refuses acceptance at the doorstep without verifiable transit damage, the ₹299 advance booking token is non-refundable to partially offset courier reverse-logistics and insurance costs.

5. Refund Process & Timelines

Upon receipt of approved returns at our atelier, our master weavers conduct a quality assurance inspection within 48 hours. Once verified:

  • Refunds are initiated directly via Razorpay back to the original source account (UPI, Credit/Debit Card, NetBanking).
  • Depending on your banking institution, funds typically reflect in your account within 5 to 7 business days.
  • For Cash on Delivery refunds, our concierge will request your verified bank account (NEFT / IMPS) details for instant direct transfer.
